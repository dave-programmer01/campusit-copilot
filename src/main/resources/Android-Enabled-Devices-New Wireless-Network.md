

Configuring Android enabled devices for the new wireless network
(Note: screenshots are from Samsung Galaxy S9+ with Firmware 4.0.4)
## 1.
On the Home/Lock Screen, slide down from the top to view your Notification
Panel and click the settings icon.


## 2.
On the Settings screen, tap Connections and turn on Wi-Fi.

## 3.
Tap the lehman_college network and proceed to type in your Lehman 360 username and
password. Be sure the EAP method is set to PEAP and the CA certificate is set to
"Don't validate". Press connect after all settings are done.
## 4.
The Wi-Fi status will display your connection to the lehman_college network.
