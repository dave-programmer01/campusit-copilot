# Setting Up Microsoft 365 (M365) MFA

Microsoft 365 MFA is required to sign in to your Lehman/CUNY email and Microsoft 365 apps (Outlook, Teams, etc.).

**IMPORTANT — this is NOT the same as CUNY Login MFA.** Microsoft 365 MFA is used only for Microsoft 365 apps like Outlook and Teams. CUNY Login MFA (a separate setup) is used for CUNYfirst, Brightspace, Navigate, Zoom, Lehman 360, and other CUNY systems. Each requires its own setup.

## Recommended: Microsoft Authenticator App

The preferred method is the **Microsoft Authenticator app** (from the Apple App Store or Google Play). If you already have Microsoft Authenticator set up for CUNY Login MFA, you can also add your Microsoft 365 account to it.

Alternative methods if you can't use the app:
- A one-time passcode from a hardware token
- SMS text messages to your phone
- A phone call to your phone

## Setting Up Microsoft MFA When Prompted

If you did not set up Microsoft MFA before it was enabled, you'll see a **"more information required"** message after entering your CUNY Login credentials to access your CUNY/Lehman email or Microsoft 365 apps. Follow the Microsoft MFA prompts to finish setup.

Official Microsoft setup guides (the steps are hosted by Microsoft):
- "Set up your Microsoft 365 sign-in for multi-factor authentication" (Microsoft support site)
- "Set up Security info from a sign-in page" (Microsoft support site)
- "How to add your accounts to Microsoft Authenticator" (Microsoft support site)
- "Set up SMS sign-in as a phone verification method" (Microsoft support site)
- "Set up a phone call as your verification method" (Microsoft support site)

## Important to Know

- When signing in to your email or Microsoft 365 apps, follow the **Microsoft MFA** prompts.
- When signing in to CUNYfirst, Brightspace, Navigate, or other CUNY systems, continue using the **CUNY Login MFA** process — not this one.
- Some students who are also CUNY employees may have already completed Microsoft MFA; if so, no additional action is needed.
- Most users only see Microsoft MFA prompts when signing in from a new device or browser.

## Keep Your Information Safe
- Never share your password or MFA passcode — Campus and CUNY IT will never ask for it.
- Do not approve an MFA prompt you did not initiate yourself.
- Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious texts or emails.
- Treat any request for your password, MFA passcode, or sensitive info as a scam.
