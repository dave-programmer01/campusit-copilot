# Resetting Your Lehman Login Password Using Recovery Email

**Objective:** This article guides users through the process of successfully resetting their forgotten Lehman Login Account Password using the Secondary Email Address (Recovery Email).

**Prerequisite:** prior Lehman Login Account activation required. Refer to the activation instructions (https://www.lehman.edu/itr/knowledge-base/lehman-login-account/activate-lehman-login-account/) to learn how to activate your account.

## Procedure

1. From your preferred browser, go to the self-service Lehman Login Account Manager (URL: https://managelogin.lehman.edu).

2. Click on the Forgot Password section, type your Lehman Login Account Username, enter the 6-digit captcha verification code shown, and click on Continue.

3. Select Secondary Email Address, and click on Continue.

4. Click on the drop-down menu and select the secondary email of choice to receive a temporary password reset link. Then, click on Continue.

5. An email describing how to reset your Lehman Login Account Password is sent to the secondary email selected. Check the inbox of your selected secondary email, open the message received and click on the link provided to reset your password.

   Note: The link provided will expire within 10 minutes. You will need to restart the Forgot Password process if the link has already expired.

6. Enter and confirm your new password. Make sure your password complies with the password guidelines displayed, and click on Reset Password.

7. A message will be displayed confirming your password has been successfully reset. You can now use your new Lehman Login Account password where applicable.
